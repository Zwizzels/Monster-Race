DataPurpose

Leaderboard: player ID, trophies and a short summary of your monster (player name, skin, stats, artifacts) To find suitable opponents. Other players see your player name and your monster as a "ghost" in races
Only if you sign in: your Unity account (Unity Player Account), e.g. your email address To secure your progress and restore it on another device or after reinstalling. The account itself is managed by Unity; we only receive confirmation of the sign-in and an account identifier
